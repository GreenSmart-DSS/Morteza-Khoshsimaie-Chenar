01

Profile

A short introduction to my research identity and motivations.

Agricultural AI researcher with expertise in climate-smart agriculture, decision support systems, and machine learning applications for water and nutrient management. My research combines data-driven modelling, environmental analytics, crop modelling and software development to improve agricultural productivity, resource-use efficiency and climate resilience.

The cornerstone of my doctoral work at the University of Tehran was the conception, development and field validation of GreenSmart-DSS — a fully integrated, AI-powered decision support system for greenhouse management. It couples a Django full-stack architecture with hybrid modelling: biophysical soil–water–plant processes combined with machine learning (Random Forest, LightGBM, Optuna) and an optimised fertigation engine, translating complex environmental and salinity data into actionable real-time irrigation and nutrient schedules. The system was validated with two years of cucumber experiments under variable saline conditions.

Beyond applied DSS development, my portfolio spans agro-hydrological system modelling (SWAP, AquaCrop, HYDRUS-1D), remote-sensing data assimilation — integrating Sentinel-2 and Landsat-8 with genetic algorithms for root-zone soil moisture estimation — and quantification of crop salinity tolerance thresholds. My approach is inherently interdisciplinary: rigorous experimental design combined with advanced analytics, feature engineering and model interpretability to deliver insights that are both scientifically robust and operationally feasible.

My long-term goal is to develop scalable digital frameworks that enhance water and nutrient use efficiency, reduce environmental footprints and support precision-farming transitions in arid and semi-arid regions globally.

Root-Zone Soil Moisture Monitoring at Field Scale

SWAP agro-hydrological model coupled with Sentinel-2 & Landsat-8 data assimilation

A framework combining the SWAP agro-hydrological model with multi-source satellite data to estimate daily root-zone soil moisture at high spatial resolution for precision irrigation. Inverse modelling and data assimilation with genetic algorithms optimise soil hydraulic parameters and incorporate vegetation indices, reducing reliance on in-situ measurements.

  • Validated across wheat and maize fields under diverse climatic and soil conditions in Iran.
  • Applies OPTRAM and optical satellite image processing for moisture and vegetation monitoring.
  • Demonstrated applicability for variable-rate irrigation to improve water use efficiency in water-scarce regions.
